Now you can just talk.

Messages are encrypted on your phone before they leave. The relay delivers them, and that is all it does with the content.

The people you checked stay checked.

An invitation connects you. It doesn't prove who someone is. Checking does, and your phone remembers.

Then you can call them.

Audio/video and secure files are implemented for verified contacts. Physical Web↔Android testing remains pending. Calls depend on your network and device.

Your phone stays yours.

The app unlocks on your phone with your device passphrase. Keep it private: a compromised phone can expose what's on it.

Desktop apps for Linux and Mac are coming. They aren't available yet.

  1. Open the downloaded APK on your Android phone.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install it, then continue.
  3. Open K3ncrypt and follow the setup. Keep your device passphrase private.

Install only the APK attached to the official release. Android may show an unfamiliar-source warning because this beta is distributed directly, not through an app store.

Where it stands

This is a private beta, still being tested. Notifications may be limited, and call quality depends on your network and device.

The service delivers messages and can see routing and connection timing. A compromised phone can expose information while it's in use. No independent audit is claimed.

Where it comes from

K3ncrypt grows out of muke1908/chat-e2ee and keeps that project's history and required notices.
