A DIFFERENT KIND OF MESSENGER

Your conversations
belong to you.

K3NCRYPT puts the tools for private communication in your hands. Messages are encrypted on your device. You decide which contacts to verify. The service helps deliver data without needing to read your conversations.

BUILT FOR PRIVATE CONVERSATION

Less exposure.
More control.

The essentials for staying in touch, designed with privacy at the center.

01 — PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

End-to-end encryption

Your message is encrypted on your device before it is sent. The service relays encrypted data and does not normally have access to message plaintext.

02 — YOUR CHOICE

Identity verification

Compare a contact’s fingerprint through another trusted channel, then confirm it yourself. An invitation alone does not make someone trusted.

03 — EVERYDAY MESSAGING

Encrypted messaging

Have one-to-one conversations with encrypted delivery, including when a contact is temporarily offline.

04 — FACE TO VOICE

Voice calls

Start a voice call with a contact after verification. Call availability depends on network and device conditions.

A CLEARER SECURITY MODEL

Privacy you can
understand.

No mysterious badges. No automatic trust. Just a few clear boundaries.

01

Encryption happens on your devices

Your client encrypts message content before delivery. The relay carries encrypted envelopes and needed routing information.

02

The relay is not your reader

The service supports delivery and availability. It is designed not to read message plaintext, though it can observe operational metadata such as connection timing and routing information.

03

Verification is explicit

Compare fingerprints with your contact using a separate trusted channel. This helps detect an unexpected identity; it is not a guarantee against every threat.
